Nickname dictionary attributions

The bundled dictionaries are normalized, deduplicated and filtered for use
in a public leaderboard. They include small manually curated additions.
They are a broad vocabulary, not a complete registry of every real name.

Popular Names by Country Dataset, sigpwned — CC0 1.0 Universal.
Localized and romanized given names and surnames, collected from Wikipedia.
https://github.com/sigpwned/popular-names-by-country-dataset
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Top English Wordlists, david47k — CC BY 3.0 Unported.
The top 10,000 lower-case noun compilation, based on the Google Ngrams
English dataset version 20120701, years 1950–2012.
Adaptations: format filtering, normalization, deduplication, exclusions and
curated additions. The original author does not endorse this website.
https://github.com/david47k/top-english-wordlists
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

random-name, Dominic Tarr — MIT. Additional given names, from the Moby
Word List by Grady Ward.
https://github.com/dominictarr/random-name
